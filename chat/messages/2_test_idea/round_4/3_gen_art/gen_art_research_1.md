## 3. Verdict

**No candidate found across 10 logged queries (20 mode-variants) and 6 fetched/grepped full papers holds localization breadth and iteration apart as independently varied, separately measured factors in MT automatic post-editing or scoped span editing.** The closest are:

- **TEaR (Wang et al., NAACL 2025 Findings)** — the single closest prior ablation.
